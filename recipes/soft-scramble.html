Soft Scrambled Eggs

Description

If you don't like scrambled eggs, you've probably never had a soft scramble like this. These are the eggs that convinced my wife I wasn't a degenerate frat boy. They're very easy and will impress your friends or family, just take your time and be patient. You got this!

Ingredients

  • Four eggs
  • 1/2 stick of butter
  • Pinch of salt and pepper
  • Chives - thinly sliced
  • Parmesan cheese - grated
  • One slice of the bread of your choice

Directions

  1. Set a medium saucepan on medium-low heat.
  2. Whisk the eggs. Add 75% of the chives and whish in.
  3. Toast your bread.
  4. Add butter to pan. When it starts to foam up, add in the eggs.
  5. Keep the eggs moving. Small curds will start to form as it heats. Keep stirring.
  6. Add in parmesan cheese to taste. Keep stirring.
  7. As the eggs cook, the mixture will appear to become thicker. Break up large curds and keep stirring.
  8. Keep cooking until texture mimics the picture above (soft, but hold it's shape on a plate).
  9. Serve over toast. Top with remaining chives and sprinkle with parmesan cheese.
  10. Enjoy!
