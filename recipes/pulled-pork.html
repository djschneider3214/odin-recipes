Pulled Pork

Description

Do you have a smoker on-hand? Of course you do! We've got a pulled pork butt recipe for you! Hosting a Super Bowl party? Trying to impress your father-in-law? Saturday in with the boys? No worries, we've got you covered.

Ingredients

  • One 13 lbs bone-in pork butt
  • Binder (we prefer yellow mustard)
  • Seasoning of choice
  • Sauce of choice

Directions

  1. Set your smoker to 250 degrees F.
  2. While the smoker is pre-heating, trim some overhanging or excess fat form the pork butt.
  3. Slather your binder onto the pork.
  4. Liberally season whole surface of the pork. Let stand 5-10 min until seasoning is "wet".
  5. Once preheated, place pork butt onto smoker fat-side down. Let smoke until desired bark is achieved.
  6. If you want to wrap, now is the time. Otherwise, continue cooking until internal temp reaches 203 and pork is probe-tender.
  7. Remove pork from moker, let sit 30-60 min to cool.
  8. Shred. Toss in the sauce of your choice until evenyl coated but not sopping wet.
  9. Enjoy!
